Refund Policy:
Customers are eligible for a prorated refund if service downtime
exceeds 24 hours. Refunds are issued as bill credits in the next
billing cycle and are subject to verification.


OUTAGE POLICY
Planned maintenance outages are communicated in advance and are not eligible for refunds.
Unplanned outages are tracked by region and time.
Only outages confirmed by the network operations team are considered valid for compensation.

ROUTER & EQUIPMENT POLICY
Router disconnections caused by customer-owned devices are not eligible for compensation.
If the router provided by the company is found to be faulty, a free replacement will be issued.
Router issues alone do not qualify for billing refunds.

MONTHLY BILLING POLICY
Monthly charges are applied for all active subscription plans regardless of usage.
Billing cycles begin on the 1st of every month.
Suspending a service mid-cycle does not pause billing unless officially requested and approved.

PAYMENT METHODS POLICY
Customers may add up to three payment methods.
Supported payment methods include Credit Cards, Debit Cards, and UPI.
Failed payments may result in temporary service suspension after a grace period of 7 days.

COMPENSATION POLICY
Compensation requests must be raised within 7 days of outage resolution.
Compensation decisions are final and based on system-verified data.
Customer support agents cannot manually override compensation outcomes.

SERVICE PLAN POLICY
Plan upgrades take effect immediately.
Plan downgrades apply from the next billing cycle.
Promotional plans are non-refundable and non-transferable.

SUPPORT TICKET POLICY
Support tickets are prioritized based on severity and region.
Open tickets must be resolved before raising compensation requests.
Duplicate tickets for the same issue may be auto-closed.

DATA ACCURACY POLICY
All decisions are based on system-logged data.
Customer-reported issues without system evidence may not be eligible for action.
